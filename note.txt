### Our SEO Approach

We combine traditional search optimization with emerging AI search strategies to help your business stay discoverable as search technology evolves.

#### 1. Technical SEO

**Build a strong technical foundation for search engines.**

Technical SEO focuses on improving how search engines discover, crawl, understand, and index your website.

- **Website Performance:** Improve loading speed and Core Web Vitals.
- **Mobile Optimization:** Ensure your website works smoothly across different devices.
- **Crawlability & Indexing:** Help search engines discover and understand your website pages.
- **Site Structure:** Organize URLs, internal links, sitemaps, and navigation.
- **Technical Audits:** Identify and address issues that may affect your website's search visibility.

**Best for:** Businesses that want a technically sound website that search engines can easily access and understand.

#### 2. On-Page SEO

**Make your content relevant, useful, and easy to understand.**

On-Page SEO focuses on optimizing individual website pages to match what your potential customers are searching for.

- **Keyword Research:** Identify relevant search terms your target audience uses.
- **Content Optimization:** Improve website copy to match search intent.
- **Title & Meta Optimization:** Create clear page titles and descriptions.
- **Heading Structure:** Organize content using relevant headings and sections.
- **Internal Linking:** Connect related pages to improve navigation and content discovery.
- **Image Optimization:** Use descriptive image names and alt text.

**Best for:** Businesses looking to improve the relevance and visibility of their existing website content.

#### 3. Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)

**Make your content easier to discover and use as an answer.**

AEO focuses on structuring your website content so search engines and answer platforms can understand it and identify useful answers to users' questions.

- **Question-Based Content:** Address common questions your customers are asking.
- **Clear Answers:** Present useful information in direct, easy-to-understand language.
- **Structured Content:** Organize information with descriptive headings, lists, and relevant sections.
- **Featured Snippet Optimization:** Improve content opportunities for direct answers in search results.
- **Structured Data:** Use relevant Schema Markup to help search engines understand your content.

**Best for:** Businesses that want their expertise and information to be easier to discover through question-based searches and answer-driven experiences.

#### 4. Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)

**Help your brand become more visible in AI-powered search.**

GEO focuses on improving how your brand and website content can be discovered, understood, and referenced by generative AI platforms such as ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini.

- **AI-Friendly Content:** Create clear, informative content that directly addresses users' needs.
- **Brand Authority:** Build a consistent and credible online presence across relevant sources.
- **Entity Optimization:** Make your brand, services, and expertise easier to identify and understand.
- **Content Credibility:** Support information with reliable sources, expertise, and relevant evidence.
- **AI Search Monitoring:** Track brand mentions, citations, and visibility across AI-powered platforms.

**Best for:** Businesses looking to strengthen their digital presence as more people use AI tools to research products, services, and companies.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**1. What is the difference between SEO, AEO, and GEO?**

SEO focuses on improving your visibility in traditional search engine results. AEO focuses on making your content easier to extract as direct answers, while GEO focuses on improving your brand's visibility and potential citations in generative AI platforms.

**2. Is SEO still important with the rise of AI search?**

Yes. Traditional SEO remains an important foundation for discoverability. Search engines and AI-powered search experiences continue to rely on accessible, relevant, and trustworthy web content.

**3. How long does SEO take to show results?**

SEO is a long-term process. Results depend on factors such as your website's current condition, competition, industry, and optimization strategy. Some improvements may appear relatively quickly, while meaningful organic growth often takes several months.

**4. Can my business appear in ChatGPT and other AI search platforms?**

Your website can become a potential source for AI-generated answers when its content is accessible, relevant, and trustworthy. However, no agency can guarantee that a specific AI platform will mention or cite your business.
